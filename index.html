About

Macroeconomics with a focus on expectations.

Before joining the Ph.D. program, I worked as a junior economist at the Bank of Korea. I study how households’ beliefs about fiscal policy shape macroeconomic outcomes, with particular attention to government debt dynamics and macroeconomic forecasting.
